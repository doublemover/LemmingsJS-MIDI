Help and shortcuts
      
This is one shared world with live game-event rhythms. Lanes and physical height regenerate the seed; speed, crews, scouts, camera and musical controls update live. Listen locally unlocks browser synthesis once. SoundFont sample loading is unavailable.

      
F1 or ? opens Help. Arrows pan; Shift makes a larger pan. Drag to pan; wheel or Z/X zoom; Shift+wheel pans. F or double-click follows the leading living actor. Space pauses; ] advances one paused tick. Backspace regenerates. +/− change speed; Shift changes five steps. Right-click volume resets to 100%; right-click speed resets to 1x.
